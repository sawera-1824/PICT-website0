A History of Maritime Connectivity

Pakistan International Container Terminal (PICT) began operations at Karachi Port in 2002 under a Build-Operate-Transfer (BOT) agreement with Karachi Port Trust (KPT). The BOT concession continued for 21 years and ended on June 17, 2023. During its operational period, PICT developed and managed container-terminal infrastructure and supported vessel, container and yard operations at Karachi Port.

PICT was associated with International Container Terminal Services, Inc. (ICTSI), a global port operator. Through its operational and technology capabilities, PICT contributed to the modernization of container-terminal processes and introduced technology-enabled services for customers and stakeholders.

PICT's historical terminal infrastructure included cargo-handling equipment, yard facilities, quay infrastructure and connectivity with transport networks. The company also documented the use of enterprise and terminal-management technologies to support its operations.
